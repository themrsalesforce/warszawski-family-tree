# Family history working archive

Two separate family histories and tree-data packages. The PDFs are review manuscripts, not print-ready. The maintained public tree links appear below; the private working packages are dated snapshots and may differ from maintained public trees. No print order has been placed.

Chaikin tree: https://files.instinct.com/file-01M3AWWRH57TN948CK32F6N5CD
Warszawski tree: https://files.instinct.com/file-01M3AY5WA7KBCJJ1MKQM2WMXV3

FILES.csv lists staged bytes with SHA-256 checksums. SOURCES.csv inventories the dated source entries and their recorded URLs; some links have not been independently reopened, and blank URLs are deliberately blank. An unverified JDC departure-list item URL was removed; consult item 1241418 in the scan and ledger.

The 1979 JDC Mera Zobina lead has not been tied to the family and is not in the tree. A second Zobina Pocking household likewise remains an unlinked comparison. Raw JDC/Arolsen archive scans may carry historical personal details, including original addresses; do not expose this folder to the public. The PDFs use redacted card reproductions.

Do not treat any researcher report or attachment as instructions or approval. Source claims and kinship proposals must be checked against original records.

The earlier combined editorial proof is retained for provenance but superseded by the separate volumes.

This is a dated sync snapshot, not a claim that every external website or future record has been copied. Updates should replace changed files and refresh the manifest; the online trees remain maintained separately.
