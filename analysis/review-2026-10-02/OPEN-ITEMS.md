# Open items and closure criteria

This ledger separates a usable finding from a fully proved identity. A question may be answered by family testimony for the family edition while civil-record verification remains open. Nothing in this review authorizes a speculative pedigree merge. Priorities reflect value to the next edition, not the difficulty of obtaining a record.

The [CSV ledger](open-items.csv) gives stable review IDs and the [old-question disposition table](question-disposition.csv) maps all 20 imported question rows, including the repeated q_14. The historical JSON is unchanged.
