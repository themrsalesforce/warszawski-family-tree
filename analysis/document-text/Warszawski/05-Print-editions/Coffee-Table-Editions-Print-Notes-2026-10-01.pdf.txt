The Chaikins
254 pages. The complete narrative, original documents, photographic album, 26 pages of
connected family charts, and 31 pages of family register. The record preserves 328 original

and uncertain links.

The Warszawskis
212 pages. All 87 substantive sections, 183 person records, 57 household groups and 131
sources are retained. Connected family trees occupy pages 50–61; detailed evidence and
source references follow the reading chapters.

Print interiors
The files marked Print-Interior retain the best source image pixels available to this edition.
Each has a Letter trim area within an 8.75 × 11.25-inch single-page PDF, including
0.125-inch bleed. Fonts are embedded, inner text margins are at least 1 inch, and actual text
glyphs stay within the half-inch trim safety area.

Reading copies
The files marked Reading-Copy are smaller Letter-size editions for convenient screen reading.
They preserve the text, page numbers and links, with raster images recompressed and capped
at 180 ppi where larger originals were available. Keep the full-source print interiors for
production.




The exact cover wrap is separate from the interior. Its spine and total dimensions require the final
binding and paper selection. No printer upload or physical proof has been completed.

Images and permissions
Source quality is preserved honestly; missing detail has not been invented


Archival image quality
Several originals are smaller than a modern 300 ppi production target. They are included
because they document the family. No image was enlarged merely to claim a higher
resolution. The two accompanying image-quality CSVs identify every placement by page,
source filename, native pixels, printed size and effective PPI.

Chaikin pages needing close image review
The placements below 150 ppi occur on pages 1, 16, 18, 22, 26, 47, 48, 52–54, 66, 77, 91,
109–112, 124 and 127–129. The full CSV also identifies the images between 150 and 300 ppi.
Larger original or licensed files can replace these later without changing the historical
account.

Warszawski pages needing close image review
The placements below 150 ppi occur on pages 1, 6, 8, 13–15, 23–24, 28, 31, 36, 39, 43, 71,
80 and 92. The original clipping and portrait pixels remain in the full-source interior; their
sharpness is limited by the source, particularly on larger image pages.

Watermarked and attributed material
JEM material remains watermarked. Chaikin: the published story on 60–61, image/story
material on 87 and 89, and the album on 163–189. Warszawski: the chapter photograph on
43 and album images on 48–49. Obtain the appropriate print files and reproduction terms
before wider distribution. The JVN portrait on Warszawski page 15 and other newspaper,
cemetery and publisher reproductions retain credits; their publication clearance has not been
certified.




A physical proof is especially important for the archival scans, the subtle ivory backgrounds and the
dark-teal photographic pages.

Before production
The remaining decisions are specific and limited


1 Choose the physical book
Confirm binding, paper, color-printing option and quantity with the printer. Use the final 254-
and 212-page interiors when obtaining exact cover templates. The title pages inside the books
are not spine-and-back-cover wraps.

2 Complete the source decisions
Review the page-specific image sheets. Decide which archival-quality images are acceptable
for the private family edition and which need better originals. Confirm reproduction terms for
licensed and credited third-party material. Preserve all watermarks until an authorized
replacement is supplied.

3 Honor the memoir review
Chaikin page 115 includes the Shelach memoir section. Permission to quote with attribution
was recorded, but the promised review by Eitan Shelach remains pending before final
publication. The book does not reproduce photographs from his memoir or claim that broader
reuse permission has been settled.

4 Approve printer and physical proofs
The interiors passed local checks for page boxes, embedded fonts, text safety, navigation,
missing glyphs and sensitive identifier patterns. Review the printer-generated PDF, then order
and inspect a physical proof before a larger run. This edition has not been certified by Lulu or
another printer.
