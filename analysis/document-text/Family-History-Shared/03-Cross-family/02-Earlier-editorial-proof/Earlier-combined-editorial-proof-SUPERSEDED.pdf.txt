OPENING




The story is still growing
    What do we carry forward when the names on a tree become voices at the
    table?


This book follows two family lines toward a shared present: the Chaikins, with roots in Jewish
communities across Eastern Europe and later work in Morocco, Scandinavia, Belgium, South Africa
and Ukraine; and the Warszawskis, whose known account begins with a postwar childhood in
Poland and a family life in Cleveland.

It is a book of people before it is a list of dates. A few lives have a written record rich enough for a
fuller scene. Others are known to us through family accounts, notices, names and relationships. The
difference matters, and this proof keeps it visible.

HOW TO READ THIS PROOF


The pages labeled “archive space” are editorial invitations, not claims that a photo exists. The tree
links point to living, updatable websites; the book itself will not stay current. Names and dates in this
proof need one final family read-through before printing.

No print order has been placed. This is an interior concept for feedback, not a Lulu-ready cover or
production file.

A branch, not a verdict

The illustrated tree on the cover is a metaphor, not a genealogical diagram. The two linked trees

On the Chaikin side: Meir Chaim Chaikin and Sarah Chana Kahanov are the paternal

Sarah’s son Yossi married Rivky Bacher, daughter of Koppel and Selma Bacher. Yossi and Rivky are

family account. Joe married Lori (Leah) Hirsch, whose parents are Larry and Ilene, called Gabby. Joe

What we can show

account, together with family accounts. These support much of the four-page Meir Chaim sequence.

hold the working source records and URLs. This proof does not reproduce those records or claim
that every online assertion is independent.

NEWLY SHARED CHART


The photographed Hebrew Lipsker fan chart was inspected. Center portraits are labeled Eliezer
Lipsker and Menucha Witkin. Most small print, names and the bottom note are too soft in the photo
to attach confidently to a person. Its large-scale structure is a research lead, not a settled branch in
this book.

Family-chat-derived details are treated as family knowledge, not literal citations to the exports. No
home addresses, phone numbers or school details are included.

PRODUCTION NOTE




The physical book

This proof is 48 square interior pages, sized 8.5 × 8.5 inches. The proposed printed object is a matte
casewrap hardcover with premium-color interior on coated paper. It is not a print order, quote or
final manufacturing file.

Lulu uses single PDF pages for a standard casewrap interior. The tree is a spread and QR-linked site,
not an integrated fold-out. A poster insert would be a separate manual step.

Before print: approve the text and audience, collect and clear photographs, verify names and dates,
finish the cover and spine template to Lulu’s current specifications, add the required bleed and safe
margins, export a press PDF, and order one proof copy only after a separate checkout approval.

WHAT THIS VERSION ALREADY DOES


It fixes the page count, square proportions and editorial rhythm. It distinguishes settled facts from
research questions and leaves honest space for the images and memories that will make this a
family book.
