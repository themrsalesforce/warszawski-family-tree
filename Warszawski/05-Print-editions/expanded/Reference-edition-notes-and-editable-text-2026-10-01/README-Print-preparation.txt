IMAGE QUALITY
Original detail has been preserved; no artificial enlargement is claimed as
new detail. Fifty-four genuine larger archived assets replaced smaller Chaikin
images. Warszawski includes original CPL notices and improved headstone scans.

Chaikin: 147 of 189 image placements are below 300 PPI; 23 are below 150 PPI.
Warszawski: 35 of 55 image placements are below 300 PPI; 15 are below 150 PPI.
These are placement counts, including repeat uses, rather than counts of unique
photographs. The two image CSVs list every affected page, native pixel dimensions,
printed size and effective PPI. Archival scans may be meaningful despite limited
detail; judge them on a physical proof or replace them with better originals.

BEFORE A PRINT RUN
Choose the binding, paper and colour option. The exact cover wrap needs those
choices and the final page count; it is a separate file from the interior.
Resolve the source-image/licensing decisions and the promised author review.
Upload the appropriate interior to the chosen printer, inspect its generated
proof, and order/review a physical proof before a larger run. This package has
not been submitted to Lulu or another printer, and no print order was placed.
