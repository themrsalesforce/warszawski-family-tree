# Larry Hersh: two newspaper versions of the Altalena story
September30,2026. Warszawski/Hersh research, not Chaikin.

## Findings
Cynthia Dettelbach's May22,2008 Cleveland Jewish News article, "Remembering the Altalena: a shipmate's tale," identifies South Euclid resident Larry Hersh as a survivor of Auschwitz, Dachau and Kaufering and a former Betar member. He describes being recruited by Kidon to smuggle arms from Czechoslovakia into the American zone of Germany for onward transport to Palestine. His group went to Marseille at the end of April1948 and loaded the ship for24 hours straight. He recalls tears, the Israeli flag and singing Hatikvah. He and four other swimmers carried a pouch ashore at Kfar Vitkin to Begin. Larry recalls Begin embracing him and saying "Welcome home," and says he never knew what the pouch held. After48 hours detained at Kfar Vitkin, he says the group entered the unified fighting force.

Ellen Harris's May15,2003 CJN article, "Area veterans of1948 war refute 'New Historians'," also reports Larry arriving on the Altalena, swimming ashore to Begin and handing over a waterproof pouch. This version calls its contents documents provided by "Stovsky" and says he almost immediately joined the Latrun fighting. It contains political and military judgments from several veterans. These are attributed opinions, not neutral quantitative findings.

## Evidence class
Two published accounts attributed to Larry. They support that he publicly told the pouch/Begin story at different times; they are not two independent witnesses. His1984 USHMM interview also says he returned on the Altalena but the examined passage does not contain the pouch episode. No passenger register was located in this research leg.

## Conflicts and corrections to carry
-2008 article says the ship raised anchor "June1,1948" on "Friday". Calculation shows June1 was Tuesday, June11 was Friday. Palmach history says June11 departure. A2024 Northern Mariner article recounting crew member James T. Williams's voyage also says FridayJune11. A dropped digit is a plausible explanation, not proved.
-2008 article dates sinking toJune21. James Williams's account and Jewish Virtual Library date the burning/sinkingJune22. Preserve the newspaper error, use sourced historical context separately.
-2008 says Larry was20 on departure;1925 birth implies23 inJune1948.2003 calls him80 inMay2003;1925 implies77 approaching78. Exact June1 birth comes from a compiled tribute, not a birth record. These inconsistent ages must not become new tree dates.
-2003: documents in pouch from "Stovsky".2008: contents unknown to Larry. Do not blend the versions.
-Passenger counts vary:2003 Larry says550 survivors,2008 says almost1,000 young people, Palmach says850, the crew account says800 men and130 women/children. These may describe different groups or approximations; no count is resolved here.
-2003 photo is captioned Zev Harel, not Larry. Never label it Larry.
