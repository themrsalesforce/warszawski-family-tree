# Larry Hersh's public voice in Cleveland
September 30, 2026. Warszawski/Hersh research.

## Original clipping: a Holocaust center proposal
Cleveland Jewish News, May 26, 2000, printed page 8, "Let's try again for center", signed Larry Hersh, Cleveland.

The writer states: "I first suggested the Cleveland Holocaust Center 25 years ago." He says Cleveland's Jewish Community Federation supported the idea, created a committee including survivors and Federation leadership, and allocated funds. He blames the project's failure on disputes among survivors over control, and urges another attempt with a committee chosen by Federation.

Evidence: the original issue PDF was retrieved. Page 8 was isolated, rendered and an enlarged 520x680 crop inspected directly. Text and author signature are legible. This proves publication of the letter and its attribution. It does not independently prove who first proposed the center, the committee's history or the accusations. The implied proposal year around1975 is arithmetic from his claim, not a record. The identity match to the interviewed Larry Hersh is contextual (name, Cleveland, Holocaust interests), not established by family identifiers in the letter.

Book use: a short attributed account of Larry's wish to build a permanent place of Holocaust memory in Cleveland. Avoid presenting him as the founder of a center. Keep his criticisms attributed, and seek Federation committee records before giving them factual narration.

Source: https://now.dirxion.com/Cleveland_Jewish_News/library/Cleveland_Jewish_News_1964-2009/Cleveland_Jewish_News_05_26_2000.pdf

## Later published letter
CJN, December 3, 2010, "Learn from the past", signed Larry Hersh, Cleveland Heights. He criticizes American Jewish attitudes toward Israel and urges learning from history. This is opinion and evidence of a public voice, not a source for neutral historical or political claims. Name/city are compatible with Larry; family identity not supplied.
