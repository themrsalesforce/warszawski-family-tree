## The act (Lisses N13/1843)
Act dated 10 June 1843 ("Du dix juin mil huit cent quarante trois"): birth of Augustine Aline ALBERT, "nee hier a neuf heures du matin" = born 9 June 1843, at the home of Jean Charles Albert, garde champetre of Lisses, her maternal grandfather (= the Charles Albert of the Fontenay acts). "Fille naturelle de Augustine Adele Albert, journaliere, agee de vingt ans, et d'un pere inconnu." Declaration by the midwife (sage-femme of Mennecy); witnesses Louis Alexandre Desire David (manouvrier, 30) and Jean Louis Jeanson (instituteur, 23).

## Margin annotations
- Recognized 4 Sept 1843.
- Legitimated by the parents' marriage, October 1843, Garson x Albert. The margin's commune word is unresolved; the lane verified the marriage is NOT in Lisses. Fontenay-le-Vicomte (the couple's later commune) is the next target.

## Conflicts kept visible
- Birth date: TD5 says 6 juin 1843; the act says born 9 June (registered 10 June). Act governs; TD value recorded as the conflicting reading.
- TD5 already lists her under "Garcon" (post-legitimation surname) although the birth act names her Albert.
- Legitimation-marriage commune: margin word unresolved; NOT Lisses; Fontenay next.
