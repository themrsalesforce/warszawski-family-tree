# Cleveland Janowitz household candidates: still unlinked
September30,2026. Warszawski/Hersh research.

## Target
Larry's1984 oral history says his maternal uncle Janowitz lived in Cleveland. An army doctor contacted him after liberation, identifying Larry as Leibi Hersch, son of the uncle's sister Leah. The uncle already corresponded with Larry's surviving sister, later sponsored her and housed Larry for three months on his1956 arrival. No first name in the examined passage.
Source: https://collections.ushmm.org/oh_findingaids/RG-50.091.0080_03_trs_en.pdf . Spoken recollection, phonetic names, not a civil record.
