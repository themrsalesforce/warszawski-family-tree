# Cleveland research, September 30, 2026: interim findings

The strongest new record lead is Rosamond Simon's January 25, 1983 Plain Dealer obituary, p2 section E. CPL identifies her as wife of Sanford. This is a strong candidate for Rochelle's late sister Rosamund Simon, but the print scan is needed before merging it. No request was sent.

Larry Hersh's young portrait is now archived. The Jewish Veterans Network profile gives June 1, 1925 - January 3, 2012 as exact dates; these are compiled tribute claims. A separate March 2013 local article quotes filmmaker Dave Beckman that Larry died "last year", supporting 2012. Exact days remain unverified. The tribute's 1949 war chronology must not displace Larry's own 1948 account.

The USHMM interview contains a family reunion story: a Jewish army doctor carried Larry's news to his Cleveland uncle, Janowitz, by locating him through the telephone directory; the uncle had already heard from Larry's surviving sister. Later the uncle paid for her immigration, took her into his family, and housed Larry for his first three months after arriving in Cleveland in 1956. Mother Leah is named. Uncle's first name remains unknown.

A 2017 Kol Israel memorial booklet offers an unlinked cluster on wall section 47: Family Hershkovitz, Jankiel Hershkovitz, Lea Herschkovitz, Faige Herschkovitz, Suri Herschkovitz, Chajah Herschkovitz, Moishe Mandel, Mindel Mandel, Esther Yanovitz. Lea and three female names are compatible with Larry's losses, but names/count alone cannot prove identity. The PDF is quarantined as an unlinked Cleveland lead, not added to the tree.

Two Sanford Simon records illustrate the risk of merging names: a 1971 man married to Helen with Jerry/Rosalyn children; a 1990 man married to late Grace G. The architectural feature about Yale graduate Sanford and son Bart is not linked to Rosamond. No claim of kinship is made.
