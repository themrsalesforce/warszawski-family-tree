## Rabbi Meyer Kogen, news obituary
Same issue, December 9, 1973. Wrapper says page 35; the reproduced print heading says 36-A. Preserve both page labels rather than silently choosing one.
Original: https://drive.google.com/file/d/12_LQ7emVvYjOI98cAbz3mwHdPZYKwQ-o/view?usp=drivesdk&authuser=shmuelsassistant%40gmail.com

Headline: "Rabbi Meyer Kogen, former principal, dies".

"Rabbi Meyer Kogen, 81, retired principal of the old Columbia Ave. branch of the Cleveland Hebrew Schools, died yesterday in Menorah Park Home for the Aged, Beachwood."

Born in Poland; came here in 1922; taught in Hebrew schools for many years before becoming principal; retired in 1960. Faculty of Cleveland College of Jewish Studies. Past president of Cleveland Hebrew Teachers Federation and Histadrut Ivrit. Author of pamphlets on prayer and the Bible. Wife Esther died in 1960. Survivors son Rabbi David C. Kogen and daughter Mrs. Marvin Spiegelman. Services 1 p.m. today at Berkowitz-Kumin funeral home, 1985 S. Taylor Rd., Cleveland Heights.

"Yesterday" in the December 9 issue yields Saturday, December 8, 1973 by calculation. The newspaper age 81 conflicts with the compiled Findagrave August 17, 1889 date relayed by the other research lane, which would imply age 84. Do not silently resolve the age conflict. "Leo" (1973) and "Arye" (1960) may be parallel names for the same deceased child, but equivalence is an inference, not established by these notices alone. None of these Kogen notices proves kinship to Elka Kogen Zelkind.
