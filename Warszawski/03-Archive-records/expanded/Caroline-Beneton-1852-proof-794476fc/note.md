# Caroline Valentine Lamperière x Louis Victor Beneton, 1852

Original reconstructed Paris marriage, old 12e, 1 April 1852. The bride was born Vernon (Eure), 11 August 1827, daughter of Jean Charles Lamperière and Rose Letellier, both deceased. Groom born Nangis, 13 June 1820, son of Jean Pierre Louis Beneton and Marie Eléonore Denise Royer.

Original bride clause: "Et de Caroline Valentine Lamperière, née à Vernon (Eure), le onze août mil huit cent vingt-sept, fille de Jean Charles Lamperière, et de Rose Letellier, son épouse, tous deux décédés."
Translation: Caroline Valentine Lamperière, born Vernon, Eure, 11 August 1827, daughter of Jean Charles Lamperière and his wife Rose Letellier, both deceased.

Supporting marriage certificate, Saint-Médard, confirms both names and parents, nuptial blessing 1 April 1852. Vernon birth bulletin repeats bride birth date and parents.

Application signature reads "Vve Beneton" (widow Beneton); the Mr/alive-husband inference in an initial report was wrong and withdrawn. Requester field is Mme Beneton, blanchisseuse, address St Jacques 29 [street prefix to corroborate], relationship field Épouse. Application year 1874; day/month uncertain.

Identity with Antoine's wife is unproven. This bride would be 37 on 15 August 1864, whereas Auguste's act says Caroline was 33. This weakens but cannot alone exclude identity. Beneton's death date remains needed. The Delphine/Jules Armand parents hypothesis receives no support from this act; this bride's parents differ.

Corrections to prior pre-1860 card report: position 21 DOES name Caroline Valentine with another spouse. Only no Antoine Joseph Garson pairing was established. No general Caroline negative may be claimed.
