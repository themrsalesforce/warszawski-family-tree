# Learn from the past

- Dec 3, 2010
- Posted Dec 3, 2010 at 12: 00 AM

Even after the Holocaust, American Jews could not, would not and did not adopt a “Never Again” attitude. Organizations such as the Irgun did not receive general Americans’ support, and organizations such as JDL were publicly condemned. The Jews of 1945-1948 Palestine were different. Their attitude was stand fast and defend. Their resolute bearing continued in 1955 and 1967 and to this day.

The American Jewish community has not changed. While rockets and explosive shells fall on Israel, American Jews, sitting safe from the shelling, engage in erudite discussion of Israeli foreign policy. And from this discussion emanates criticism of Israeli foreign policy, which American Jews seem to feel they must promulgate. The discourse has reached the level where Christian organizations such as CUFI, Christians United for Israel, via their pro-Israeli stance appear pro-Jewish, and Jewish organizations such as J Street sound anti-Semitic.

In the early 20th century, Georges Santayana wrote, “Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it.” These words have been paraphrased, “Those who do not study and learn from their past are doomed to repeat it.” I hope and pray the words are not prophetic.

Larry Hersh, Cleveland Heights
