## Interpretation
The independently checked 9e-20e bride bands fill the remaining bride-side coverage gap for the 1860-1872 window. Earlier blocks checked 1e-8e bride bands and groom bands. Those earlier searches remain separate evidence; this package does not claim that each full register was searched line by line.
Auguste's birth act explicitly says "Mariés à la Cinquième Mairie en mil huit cent soixante" (Married at the fifth town hall in 1860). The date/place citation remains in conflict with the bounded index searches. Possible indexing error, wrong year, locality outside Paris, or an erroneous birth-act citation remain hypotheses, not findings.
No publication, tree merge, family contact, or purchase was made.
