# Pre-1860 Paris Garson cards

Original scans visually inspected 30 September 2026.

## Birth lead

V3E/N965, position 34: "Garson / Antoine Joseph / 6 8bre 1832", old 11e. Translation: Antoine Joseph Garson, born 6 October 1832 in old 11th arrondissement. Identity with Caroline's husband remains unproved.

Source: https://archives.paris.fr/_recherche-images/show/588831/image/337971/34

## Marriage band

V3E/M414: positions 12-18 inspected as full cards. 12 Garsin boundary, 13-17 Garson, 18 Garsonnat boundary. No Antoine Joseph Garson x Caroline Lamperière. This is a bounded restored-card index negative, not evidence no marriage occurred.

Entries: 13 Schayé [reading uncertain] Garson x Esther Nessely, 1 brumaire an 6; 14 Pierre Auguste x Marie Marguerite Hervé, 26 ventôse an 9; 15 Charles Jean x Marie Anne Catherine Craïé [surname reading uncertain], 23 August 1816; 16 Victor René x Hélène Hembacher, 3 June 1830; 17 César Marc x Henriette Adeline Debressenne [reading], 11 7bre 1849 (11 September 1849).

No relationship of these marriages to target assumed.
