# Unlinked Huszt Herskovitz family: WWI welfare inquiry
NARA RG59, NAID286801887, decimal file364.64/239, images122-124.
https://catalog.archives.gov/id/286801887?objectPage=122

January25,1917 American Consulate General Budapest report to State Department: Mr. and Mrs. Hers Herskovitz, Huszt, Maramaros County, all his family and child Emil Wiessel in good health. Refers to instruction631, December19,1916, file364.64/178, not yet located.
March14,1917 Department reply to Miss Matilda Herskovitz, care of Mr. T. Gans,328East79thStreet,NewYorkCity. Says her father and mother remain at address she supplied; family and child Emil are well.

Original visual checks: all3pages legible. Initial in care-of address read T., correcting an earlier R. transcription. The child surname differs slightly between letters; no identity asserted. No evidence links this family to Larry Hersh or Leah Janowitz. This is a research lead, not an ancestor profile. Do not imply an Elie Wiesel relationship.
