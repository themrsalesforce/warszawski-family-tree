"Judith Dratler (nee Herskowitz), 96, passed away on June 5, 2025.
Judith was the beloved wife of the late Phillip Dratler, and mother of Leigh (Mark) Wasserman and Jay Dratler. She was a cherished grandmother to Erin (Jesus) Rosado and Michelle Wasserman, and a loving great-grandmother to Jacob, Eva and Ori.
Judith was predeceased by her dear brother, Lawrence Hersh, and is lovingly remembered alongside her parents and siblings who perished in the Holocaust.
Private funeral services were held for immediate family at Bet Olam Cemetery in Beachwood.
In lieu of flowers, donations may be made to the Kol Israel Foundation (kifcle.org).
Expressions of sympathy and memories may be shared at bkbmc.com for the Dratler family.
Arrangements are under the care of Berkowitz-Kumin-Bookatz Memorial Chapel."
